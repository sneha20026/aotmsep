What is Solar Energy?

Solar energy is energy obtained from the Sun. It is one of the most widely used renewable energy sources. Solar panels use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electrical energy. Unlike fossil fuels, sunlight is naturally replenished, making solar power an important part of a sustainable energy system.

How Does Solar Energy Work?

☀️

1. Sunlight

Sunlight reaches the surface of the solar panels.

🔆

2. Solar Panels

Photovoltaic cells capture sunlight and produce electrical energy.

⚡

3. Electricity

The generated electricity can be converted and supplied to electrical devices.

🏠

4. Use Energy

Solar electricity can power lights, appliances, machines and other devices.
