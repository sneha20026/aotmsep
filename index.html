What is Sustainable Energy?

Sustainable energy is energy produced and used in a way that meets our present needs without harming the ability of future generations to meet their needs. Renewable sources such as sunlight, wind, water and biomass can provide cleaner alternatives to fossil fuels. Sustainable energy can help reduce pollution, protect natural resources and fight climate change.
